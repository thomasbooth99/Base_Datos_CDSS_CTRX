LEAP 2015 - GWU - Laboratory Experiments									
									
This database contains the results of laboratory experiments for the characterization of Ottawa F65 Sand. The experiments are part of the LEAP 2015 project performed at the George Washington University Soil Mechanics Laboratory.  									
									
The Database contains the following:									
1. A report of characterization tests results on Ottawa F65 sand including:					
	"Specific Gravity, Particle Size Distribution, Permeability, maximum and minimum void ratio tests"								
2. "The results of a series of monotonic triaxial compression and extension tests performed at three different void ratios (0.724, 0.604, 0.584)"
								
3. The results of a series of cyclic triaxial tests in strain- and stress-controlled conditions at the void ratio of 0.604
